1. ATTENDANCE & WORKING HOURS POLICY

1.1 Purpose

This policy establishes the company's requirements concerning working hours, attendance, absences, overtime, and leave administration to ensure operational efficiency, employee accountability, and fairness.

1.2 Scope

This attendance policy applies to all white-collar employees unless otherwise specified in their employment agreements or approved departmental policies.

1.3 Standard Working Hours

  • The standard working requirement is 40 hours per week, based on five working days.
  • Employees may follow flexible working schedules according to departmental requirements and subject to management approval.
  • Flexible working arrangements do not exempt employees from mandatory core attendance hours.
  • Employees are responsible for completing their required weekly working hours.
  • All working hours must be accurately recorded through the company's approved attendance tracking system.

1.4 Mandatory Core Attendance Hours

  • All employees must be present during the mandatory core hours of 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM on each scheduled working day.
  • Arriving after 10:00 AM or leaving before 2:00 PM requires authorization.
  • Exceptions must be supported by an approved day-off or time-off request submitted by email to the direct manager, with HR copied.
  • Requests should preferably be submitted in advance unless an emergency prevents prior notification.

1.5 Monthly Working Hours Shortage

  • Employees are permitted a maximum monthly working hours shortage of 8 hours without an attendance-shortage deduction.
  • If the total monthly shortage exceeds 8 hours, the entire shortage will be considered for deduction, not merely the hours exceeding the permitted allowance.
  • Deductions will be calculated in complete blocks of 8 shortage hours, equivalent to one working day.
  • Any remaining shortage hours that do not constitute a complete 8-hour block will not result in an additional day deduction.
  • The calculated deduction will first be applied against the employee's available annual leave balance, subject to applicable law.
  • Once the employee's annual leave balance is fully consumed, any further deductible days will be deducted from the employee's monthly salary, in accordance with applicable labor laws.
  • The monthly shortage allowance is not cumulative and cannot be transferred to subsequent months.
  • The monthly shortage allowance does not exempt employees from mandatory core attendance requirements.

Calculation Rule:

If total monthly shortage is 8 hours or less, no deduction applies.

If total monthly shortage exceeds 8 hours, the deduction is calculated by rounding down the total shortage hours to complete multiples of 8, with a minimum deduction of one day.

Example: An employee with 20 shortage hours and an available annual leave balance of 1 day will have 1 day deducted from annual leave and 1 day deducted from salary.

1.6 Overtime and Off-Site Assignments

  • All overtime work and off-site assignments must receive prior written approval from the employee's direct manager.
  • Approval must be communicated by email, with HR copied where required.
  • Unauthorized additional working hours will not automatically qualify for compensatory leave, subject to statutory requirements.
  • Approved excess working hours may be converted into compensatory leave and added to the employee's leave balance, provided the arrangement complies with applicable labor laws.
  • Compensatory leave credits will be calculated in complete blocks of 8 approved hours, equivalent to one working day.
  • Approved excess hours that are not an exact multiple of 8 will be rounded down to the nearest complete 8-hour block for internal compensatory leave purposes.
  • Statutory overtime compensation rights remain applicable regardless of the internal leave-credit mechanism.
  • HR shall maintain records of approved overtime, off-site assignments, and resulting leave credits.

1.7 Absence and No-Show Policy

  • Employees unable to attend work must notify their direct manager and submit a day-off request by email, with HR copied.
  • Where advance notice is not possible, the employee must submit the request within 48 hours of the missed working day.
  • Failure to submit the required request within 48 hours will result in the missed day being provisionally recorded as an unauthorized absence, subject to review of legitimate emergencies and legally protected circumstances.
  • Unauthorized absences will be handled under the company's disciplinary procedures and applicable labor laws.
  • Repeated unauthorized absences may result in progressive disciplinary action, following the required procedures.

1.8 Attendance Monitoring

  • HR is responsible for maintaining accurate attendance and leave records.
  • Employees must regularly review their attendance records through the company portal.
  • Any discrepancies must be reported promptly to HR.
  • Direct managers are responsible for reviewing and approving attendance exceptions within their departments.

2. EMPLOYEE LIFE EVENT AWARDS POLICY

2.1 Purpose

IENERGY METERS MANUFACTURING recognizes significant personal milestones and provides financial support to employees during important family events.

2.2 Award Entitlements

OccasionAward Amount
Employee MarriageEGP 2,000
Birth of a ChildEGP 3,000
Death of a First-Degree Relative or SpouseEGP 4,000

2.3 Marriage Award

  • Employees who legally marry during their employment are eligible for a marriage award of EGP 2,000.
  • The marriage award may be granted a maximum of two times during the employee's employment with the company.
  • An official marriage certificate must be submitted to HR.
  • The award will be paid with the next monthly salary following submission and verification of the required documents.

2.4 Childbirth Award

  • Employees becoming a father or mother are eligible for EGP 3,000 per qualifying birth event.
  • The award applies equally to eligible male and female employees.
  • An official birth certificate or birth notification must be submitted to HR.
  • Where both parents are employed by the company, each eligible employee may receive the award.
  • The award will be paid with the next monthly salary following submission and verification of the required documents.

2.5 Bereavement Support Award

  • Employees experiencing the death of a qualifying first-degree relative or spouse are entitled to financial support of EGP 4,000 per qualifying event.
  • Qualifying relatives include parents, children, and spouses.
  • An official death certificate or other acceptable documentation must be submitted to HR.
  • The award will be paid with the next monthly salary following submission and verification of the required documents.

2.6 General Award Conditions

  • All awards are available to eligible active employees following HR verification.
  • All approved financial awards shall be paid together with the next monthly salary after submission and verification of the required supporting documents.
  • No separate advance payment shall be made unless specifically approved by the CEO.
  • Life-event awards are separate from employees' contractual base salaries.
  • Applicable tax and payroll treatment shall follow Egyptian law.
  • Award amounts may be reviewed periodically by management.

3. SPECIAL PAID LEAVE POLICY

3.1 Purpose

The company provides additional paid leave for important personal and family occasions as part of its employee welfare program.

These benefits are intended to supplement statutory leave entitlements, not reduce or replace them.

3.2 Special Leave Entitlements

Leave TypeEntitlement
Marriage Leave6 paid working days
Childbirth Leave — Fathers3 paid working days per birth event (company benefit)
Childbirth Leave — Mothers4 paid months, subject to applicable labor law
Bereavement Leave3 paid working days

3.3 Marriage Leave

  • Employees are entitled to 6 paid working days upon their marriage.
  • Marriage leave may be granted a maximum of two times during the employee's employment with the company, consistent with the marriage award eligibility limit.
  • The leave must be requested through the company's approved leave procedure.
  • Employees should submit the request in advance, with supporting documentation provided to HR.
  • Marriage leave is separate from the employee's annual leave balance.
  • Marriage leave cannot be converted into cash.
  • Unused marriage leave cannot be carried forward.

3.4 Childbirth Leave

  • Fathers are entitled to 3 paid working days upon the birth of their child, per birth event, as a company benefit.
  • The father's 3 days may be taken consecutively or separately during the month in which the child is born, with scheduling coordinated with the direct manager and without limiting statutory birth-day leave.
  • Mothers are entitled to 4 months of paid maternity leave under Article 54 of Egyptian Labor Law No. 14 of 2025. This includes leave before and after childbirth, with at least 45 days after delivery, and is available up to three times during employment.
  • Mothers must provide HR with a medical certificate stating the expected delivery date.
  • Childbirth leave requests must be submitted through the company's approved leave procedure. The direct manager coordinates coverage, and HR verifies eligibility; administrative approval must not restrict statutory maternity leave.
  • Childbirth leave does not reduce the employee's annual leave balance.
  • Unused company-granted leave for fathers expires at the end of the child's birth month and cannot be carried forward or converted into cash. This expiry does not apply to statutory maternity leave or limit any statutory entitlement.

3.5 Bereavement Leave

  • Employees are entitled to 3 paid working days following the death of a qualifying family member.
  • Qualifying family members include parents, children, and spouses.
  • The employee must inform the direct manager and HR as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Supporting documentation may be submitted after the employee returns to work.
  • Bereavement leave is separate from annual leave and does not reduce the employee's annual leave balance.
  • This company benefit shall not reduce any greater leave entitlement required by applicable law.

3.6 Special Leave Administration

  • Special leave requests shall be recorded through the company's approved leave management system.
  • The direct manager is responsible for coordinating operational coverage.
  • HR is responsible for verifying eligibility and maintaining leave records.
  • Special leave cannot be converted into financial compensation unless expressly required by law.

4. EMPLOYEE LOANS POLICY

4.1 Purpose

This policy provides short-term financial support to employees through company loans and establishes clear eligibility, approval, repayment and guarantee requirements to ensure consistent administration and responsible lending.

4.2 Eligibility

  • Employees may apply for a loan only after successfully completing their probation period.

4.3 Loan Amount and Repayment

  • The maximum loan amount is one month of the employee's net salary.
  • The loan must be repaid in full through monthly installments within 6 months of disbursement or by 31 December of the calendar year in which it is disbursed, whichever comes first.
  • Installments are deducted directly from the employee's monthly salary according to the signed repayment schedule.
  • The repayment schedule must ensure that no outstanding loan balance is carried into the next calendar year.
  • Loans are interest-free. Loan agreements and salary deductions must comply with applicable law.

4.4 Colleague Guarantee

  • Before disbursement, another colleague must sign the loan agreement as guarantor, accepting responsibility for the outstanding balance if the borrowing employee fails or becomes unable to repay, including following termination of employment.
  • The guarantor's repayment responsibility and the circumstances in which it applies must be stated in the signed agreement. Any recovery from the guarantor must follow that agreement and applicable law.

4.5 Approval

  • Every loan request must be approved by the employee's direct manager before the loan is disbursed.
  • The employee must sign a loan agreement stating the approved amount, repayment schedule and authorization for salary deductions.
